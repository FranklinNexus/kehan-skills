# Principles

This file is for humans. `skills/` is for AI agents.

## Lens

Kehan / 董科含 is compressed here into: early-stage founder coaching, fundraising, young-founder judgment, investor selection, and AI-era startup execution.

## Core Principles

- Financing is a tool for acceleration, not validation.
- The founder is one of the earliest products: mission fit, credibility, learning rate, and momentum matter.
- Investor praise is weak until it becomes a concrete next step.
- Terms, cap table, and control can matter more than headline valuation.
- Young founders should seek safe aggression: reversible experiments before irreversible life bets.
- AI lowers build cost, so judgment, distribution, and structure matter more.
- Coffee chats become valuable only when converted into tracked commitments, evidence, and follow-up metrics.

## Practical Standard

A good answer using this lens should:

- Name the real problem type.
- Separate evidence from story.
- Identify the bottleneck.
- Push back when the requested tactic does not match the real issue.
- Give a concrete next action.
- Avoid roleplay and imitation.
